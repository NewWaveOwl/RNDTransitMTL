## 1. Team Decision-Making Framework

### Approach

The team will aim to reach consensus through discussion. Each member will always have an opportunity to present their reasoning before the team makes any decision.

### Decision-Making Technique

Any decision made will have to be unanimous and will require discussion until all teammates have fully understood all the arguments and ideas presented. This ensures that every team member understands the strengths and weaknesses of all the ideas and agrees on what will be the best decision for any given problem.

### Handling Disagreements

If, for some reason, even after extensive discussion, the team members have different visions for the project, the team will resort to a majority vote. If every team member disagrees, the team will consult an expert or external consultant to help determine the best direction for the team. In this case, the consultant will have the final vote.

## 3. Team Norms

The following agreements define how we will communicate, collaborate, and remain accountable.

1. **Communication Platform:** All communication will be done primarily through Discord or Teams if there are any issues with Discord.

2. **Response Delays:** Expected response times are between 4 and 6 hours. If there is no deadline or urgency, a delay of up to 24 hours will be tolerated before any further measures are taken.

3. **Attendance:** Team members will attend every class where milestone work is going to be done, unless there is a valid reason not to do so. If possible, absent team members will have to attend online. At a minimum, they are responsible for being available to communicate during work sessions to answer any questions.

4. **Respect:** Team members must remain respectful throughout every interaction. Our team will have no tolerance for disrespect. Failure to abide by this policy will result in serious discussions and arrangements for exclusion depending on the severity of the situation.

5. **Progress Updates:** Team members will be responsible for announcing any work completed to keep the team up to date.

## 4. Preliminary AI Usage Policy

### Permitted Use

AI will be used for documentation, troubleshooting, boilerplate code, and architecture/design consultation. If any other additional help is needed, AI will always be a valid option to rely on.

### Restricted / Prohibited Uses

AI should not be used for:

- Writing code that the person using the AI does not understand.

> Any further restrictions will be discussed throughout the milestones.

### Required Human Review

Members are expected to use AI responsibly. Any AI-generated code or content must be reviewed and understood by a team member before being included in the project. The team member using the output remains responsible for its correctness.

All significant AI usage will be documented using an AI log (`AI_USAGE.md`).

### Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed and updated:

- At the end of each sprint.
- When the teacher provides feedback.
- When the team's use of AI changes.
