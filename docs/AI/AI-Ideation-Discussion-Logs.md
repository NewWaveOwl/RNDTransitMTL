## Prompt 1: Reframe as a user need

**Prompt**

> Can you word this as a "user need"?
>
> You want to go on a trip. You look up some ideas. You have lists upon lists of results.
> Endless destinations on trip advisor, and near-infinite locations on google maps. You're paralyzed by choice. Everything feels too planned, and choosing a place to go sounds like even more effort than actually going there.

**Output**

You need spontaneity. You need something to tell you where to go, a quest to follow.

### Standard product framing (user story)

> "As a travel seeker overwhelmed by excessive research and choice paralysis, I need a frictionless way to receive spontaneous destination quests, so that I can experience the excitement of travel without the burden of planning."

### Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framing

> "When I want to take a trip but feel drained by endless lists and over-curated itineraries, help me surrender the decision-making to a spontaneous prompt or challenge, so I can rediscover the thrill of unexpected adventure."

### Problem-statement framing (design thinking)

> "Travelers need an effortless, guided catalyst for spontaneous exploration because information overload turns destination planning into an exhausting chore rather than an exciting journey."

## Prompt 2: Existing random-trip apps

**Prompt**

> are there any apps that genearte random trips for random adventures either local are travel

**Output**

*Are there any apps that generate random trips for random adventures either local or travel?*

Yes, there are several apps that can help you generate random trips for adventures, whether local or travel-related. Here are some of the best options available:

1. **Travel Roulette:** Pick your interests and region, then roll the dice to find a random destination. It also offers a variety of other random generators for fun.
2. **GoRandomly:** An AI-powered travel adventure generator that turns your vibe, budget, and timing into a playful travel idea in under a minute.
3. **Random Trip Generator:** Helps you pick a destination by continent, budget, and travel style, with an interactive map and cost estimates.
4. **GoTripzi:** A random country and destination generator that lets you spin the globe and land somewhere new, with options to filter by budget, climate, and more.

These apps can be a great way to discover new places and experiences, whether you're looking for a local adventure or a travel destination. Just remember to check the details and plan accordingly for your trip.
